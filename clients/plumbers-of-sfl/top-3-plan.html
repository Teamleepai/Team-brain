Audit section 04 XML Sitemaps Critical

You handed Google two different maps to the same house.

You mailed the delivery driver two maps of your neighborhood, and they don't match. So he stops trusting both of them and just guesses.

What's broken

Two plugins are each publishing their own sitemap — Jetpack makes /sitemap.xml, Rank Math makes /sitemap_index.xml — and both are announced to Google. There's a third for news you don't publish.

What it costs you

Google splits its attention across three conflicting lists, crawls the wrong URLs, and gives up before finding your money pages. This is the #1 reason only 6 of your pages are indexed.

What we do

  • Turn Jetpack's sitemap off entirely
  • Keep Rank Math as the single source of truth
  • Delete the news sitemap (you're not a newsroom)
  • Resubmit one clean sitemap in Search Console and Bing
PROOF FROM YOUR AUDIT: robots.txt declares three sitemaps — /sitemap.xml, /news-sitemap.xml, /sitemap_index.xml. The news sitemap contains only your homepage. Two competing sitemap systems are live at the same time.

24/7 hours + AI agent Why this is a ranking play, not a staffing decision

"Open now" is a ranking factor. You're closed most of the day.

When someone searches at 2am, Google tries hard to only show places that are actually open. If your hours say closed, you're not in the running — no matter how good your website is.

The three things 24/7 hours unlock

1. You become eligible to appear for every overnight and weekend search — historically the least competitive, highest-urgency, highest-ticket window of the week.

2. Three of the ten keywords we're guaranteeing contain the words "emergency" or "24 hour." Those are unwinnable with 9-to-5 hours listed.

3. Emergency calls average roughly $450 versus about $275 for a routine call. The overnight window isn't just extra volume — it's better volume.

The rule you cannot break

Google's guidance is explicit: do not list 24/7 hours if nobody is reachable 24/7. Google cross-references your other listings and directories for consistency, and customers report businesses that don't answer.

So we don't flip the hours first and hope. We build the AI agent first, prove it answers, and only then change the hours. That order is non-negotiable and it's written into the contingencies.

SEQUENCING: AI agent live and tested in week 2 → hours flipped to 24/7 in week 3 → emergency keywords enter the guarantee basket in week 4. Flipping hours early is the single fastest way to get a profile suspended.
