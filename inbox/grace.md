## Division A — who you're calling

Independent auto repair shops, 1–3 locations, 4+ bays, 3+ technicians. **Owner-operated** — the main
line rings to a mobile, which is why connect rates are high on this list.

## The opener is not a pitch

Alec writes it in week 2, but the shape is fixed: **read one true thing back to them, then ask a
question.** Every prospect on your list has a verified signal attached — a review complaining nobody
called back, a service-advisor job posting open 40 days, an off-hours call that went to voicemail.

The arithmetic that lands: **25–45 calls a day is typical for a shop their size, 20–30% get missed, and
85% of people who hit voicemail never call back.** Compute it with them from their own numbers. **Never
quote the $75,000/month figure** — it's an industry ceiling, not their number, and a good owner will
challenge it.
