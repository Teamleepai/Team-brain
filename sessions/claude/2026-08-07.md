**The finding:** every earlier doc assumed 2 callers at 40/day and treated dial capacity as binding. Doc
`03`'s Salesloft council said the missing lever was a dedicated dialer — **they already had two.** Lead
flow is not the constraint; **delivery capacity and closer calendar are.**

**Strategic consequence:** surplus dial capacity is permission to raise price, because you can afford to
lose more deals and the same delivery load yields more revenue. Revised target from 17 customers at
$2,735 to **14 at $3,343 blended** (6 Group @ $5,000 + 8 Fast @ $2,100 = $46,800) — **18% less delivery
load for identical revenue.**

B = med spa 3–8 locations, Aaron+Alec dial, $5,000), 2-week data sprint then 14 selling weeks, 1 close
per week, Friday scorecard, build freeze 1 October, last Division B start ~1 October.

**Unblock found:** cold calling does not need Gate 1 — human-dialed B2B calls are legal without prior
consent. **Only SMS and email need the consent gate.** Dialing starts week 3 regardless of the attorney
timeline. This was ambiguous in earlier docs and it de-risks the schedule materially.
